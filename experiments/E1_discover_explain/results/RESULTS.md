# E1 result: Discover–Explain

This experiment uses the frozen RPIA constant-cement Scheme 1 forward model, the 59 selected Hugin samples, fixed target scales `diag(0.015, 0.20)`, transfer-aware data covariance, and the original unit-Gaussian nuisance priors.

## Main result

At the pooled RPIA operating point (`Vcem=1.255%`, `Cn=5.436`), the empirical identifiable-coordinate slope is reproduced by the complete physics:

- raw eigen-direction: **20.274**;
- nuisance-adjusted eigen-direction: **19.989**;
- median `Vp` sensitivity ratio: **20.069**;
- median `Vs` sensitivity ratio: **20.290**.

The contact endpoint alone is steeper (`beta_Kb=24.51`, `beta_Gb=24.00`). Propagation through the constant-cement HS path lowers the dry-modulus slopes to `beta_Kd=19.59` and `beta_Gd=20.29`. The median decomposition is `24.38 + (-4.76) = 19.59` for bulk modulus and `24.02 + (-3.74) = 20.29` for shear modulus.

Therefore, **20.3 is physically explainable but not universal**. Across the complete calibration box, the raw eigen-slope spans `0.16` to `311.22`, and the adjusted slope spans `-0.13` to `311.45`. Within the transfer-supported practical cells, the raw range is narrower: `5.71` to `8.12`.

## Separability result

The pooled adjusted minimum information is `0.003498`, the adjusted spectral ratio is `0.0004253`, and the angular-independence factor is `0.001994`. Thus the full model is formally rank two, but the second direction remains extremely weak after nuisance adjustment.

Density has zero direct target sensitivity in this forward model. It can change adjusted information only by constraining nuisances; this is verified in `E1_observable_staircase.csv`.

## Finite-ridge result

Near the pooled point, the RMS discrepancy in `ln Cn` between the raw structural profile and the local 20.3 exponential is `0.01197`. Integrating the spatially varying raw slope reduces this to `0.005099`. The nuisance-profiled structural ridge versus the integrated adjusted slope has local RMS `0.005071`.

The sensitivity decomposition gives `beta_contact=24.120` and `beta_HS=-3.849` in the raw metric. Approximating these terms as `A/Vcem` and `-Gamma/phi_b` gives `A=0.30261` and `Gamma=1.49118`, and therefore the physics-factored coordinate

`q_phys = ln(Cn/Cn0) + A ln(Vcem/V0) + Gamma ln(phi_b/phi_b0)`.

Its exponential remains between `1.0000` and `1.0180` along the complete raw structural ridge. The nuisance-adjusted version remains between `1.0000` and `1.0185` along the nuisance-profiled ridge. By contrast, the local 20.3 exponential varies from `1.000` to `2.001`. The simpler `-B(Vcem-V0)` term is retained in the tables as the local linearization of `Gamma ln(phi_b/phi_b0)`.

As an internal cross-trajectory check, the independently calibrated 19A and BT2 optima give pooled physics-coordinate ratios `1.0118` and `0.9959`, respectively. Their local 20.3 ratios are `1.1048` and `1.2493`. Independently recomputed raw coefficients are `A=0.2975, Gamma=1.4565` for 19A and `A=0.3110, Gamma=1.5256` for BT2. This supports Hugin-level transferability, but remains an internal validation within the same constitutive model.

The strongest interpretation supported by E1 is therefore: static elastic data identify a **nonlinear constant-cement trajectory coordinate**, jointly generated by cemented-contact stiffness and the HS porosity path. The physics-factored expression is a strong approximate invariant for this experiment, but its coefficients remain operating-point, weighting, and model dependent; it is not yet a universal material state. The residual second direction is too weak to treat `Vcem` and `Cn` as independently recovered microstructural parameters.

Along the full raw structural ridge, the centered local empirical coordinate varies from `1.000` to `2.001` and the contact radius from `0.442` to `1.582` of its pooled value. In contrast, the median dry shear modulus remains between `1.00000` and `1.00206`. This is direct evidence that the finite invariant is a compensated trajectory through contact and HS state, not either nominal parameter or the contact endpoint alone.
