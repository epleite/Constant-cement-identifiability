# Historical provisional manuscript outline

## Working title

**Can pressure break the cement-volume–coordination-number ambiguity?
Nuisance-adjusted experimental design for the constant-cement model**

## Central thesis

Static elasticity observes an effective constant-cement trajectory state.
Multi-pressure observations do not recover the nominal microstructure unless
their new sensitivity remains independent after compliant- and stiff-fabric
adjustment.

## Structure

1. **Introduction**
   - E1/E2 established the stable `q_star` coordinate and nominal ambiguity.
   - Observable count is not sensitivity independence.
   - Question: can pressure provide an adjusted independent direction?

2. **Exact pressure no-go for frozen constant cement**
   - Code/equation audit.
   - Replication versus constitutive diversity.

3. **Prospective pressure scenario and inverse design**
   - Bounding-average extension anchored at 39 MPa.
   - Fabric-link hierarchy.
   - Differential covariance, Schur adjustment, E-optimality.

4. **Results**
   - Shared-fabric analysis appears to break the ridge.
   - Expanded fabric nuisances collapse the gain from 634× to 3.35×.
   - Absolute uncertainty and profile diagnostics show persistence of the ridge.
   - Target-aligned discrepancy reduces residual gain to 1.21×.
   - Reference-pressure, trajectory, operating-point, and cost controls.

5. **Discussion**
   - Why fabric sharing masquerades as experimental information.
   - Implications for laboratory design and hierarchical targets.
   - Limits of the bounding-average scenario and convex validity domain.
   - Required independent fabric constraint or alternative pressure physics.

6. **Conclusions**
   - Pressure sensitivity is necessary but not sufficient.
   - Remediation must survive nuisance adjustment in the enlarged fabric space.
